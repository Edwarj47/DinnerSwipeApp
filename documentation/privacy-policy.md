# Dinner Swipe Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

Dinner Swipe is provided by Data Centric Software Solutions LLC. This policy adapts the existing DCSS website privacy approach for the Dinner Swipe meal-planning application.

## Information We Collect

Dinner Swipe may collect account information, profile preferences, household and group membership, recipe data you create or import, recipe URLs you submit, uploaded photos, weekly meal plans, grocery list items, pantry exclusions, and support communications.

## Recipe And Web Ingestion Data

When you submit a public recipe URL, Dinner Swipe fetches and analyzes the page to create a reviewable recipe candidate. Source URLs, source titles, detected images, extraction warnings, confidence metadata, and approval history may be stored to preserve attribution and provenance.

## Group Voting

Members of a dinner group can vote on recipe ideas. Group owners may see vote summaries, percentages, counts, winners and non-winning choices, and which member voted for each visible choice. Zero-count choices are not shown in owner vote detail charts.

## Preferences And Allergens

Cooking preferences, disliked ingredients, and allergens are stored per user. These settings are intended to help filter and annotate meal planning, but Dinner Swipe is not a medical, allergy, or nutrition safety system.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to DSAsupport@dcss.dev.
