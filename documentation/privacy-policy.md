# Dinner Swipe Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

The maintained full policy is available at `https://dinner.dcss.dev/privacy`.
This repository summary does not replace that full policy or the store's
Data Safety / App Privacy disclosures.

Dinner Swipe is provided by Data Centric Software Solutions LLC. This policy adapts the existing DCSS website privacy approach for the Dinner Swipe meal-planning application.

## Information We Collect

Dinner Swipe may collect account information, profile preferences, household and group membership, recipe data you create or import, recipe URLs you submit, uploaded photos, weekly meal plans, grocery list items, pantry exclusions, and support communications.

We record meal choices (plan, favorite, skip, and hide), when they happen, and when a plan is undone to provide weekly picks and understand product usage. These choices are included in your account data export.

## Recipe And Web Ingestion Data

When you submit a public recipe URL, Dinner Swipe fetches and analyzes the page to create a reviewable recipe candidate. Source URLs, source titles, detected images, extraction warnings, confidence metadata, and approval history may be stored to preserve attribution and provenance.

## AI Recipe Processing

When you choose Create draft in Add with AI, Dinner Swipe sends the description and photo you provide to OpenAI to generate a recipe or read a nutrition label. We save the resulting draft, including any extracted nutrition, until you save or discard it, and retain generation status and usage records. The input photo is not added to your recipe or retained in Dinner Swipe's recipe-photo storage. Photos you separately attach to a recipe are stored as recipe photos. OpenAI processes submitted content under its applicable data-processing and retention policies; Dinner Swipe does not promise zero retention by the provider. Avoid submitting personal or sensitive information that is not needed for your recipe.

## Group Voting

Members of a dinner group can vote on recipe ideas. Group owners may see vote summaries, percentages, counts, winners and non-winning choices, and which member voted for each visible choice. Zero-count choices are not shown in owner vote detail charts.

## Preferences And Allergens

Cooking preferences, disliked ingredients, and allergens are stored per user. These settings are intended to help filter and annotate meal planning, but Dinner Swipe is not a medical, allergy, or nutrition safety system.

## Subscription And Billing Data

Dinner Swipe may store subscription status, plan tier, billing provider identifiers, billing events, trial timing, and support records. Payment cards and checkout are handled by the billing provider and are not stored directly by Dinner Swipe.

## Macro Tracking And Workout Records

Macro tracking stores meal dates, portions, labels, notes, targets and saved
calculation references. Manually entered nutrition can be retained; returned
FatSecret nutrition is temporary. Workout templates, plans and performance logs
can include exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, durations, completion and
notes. These records may reveal health and fitness information. Personal
performance logs are account-specific; deliberately shared templates and group
planning choices are visible to the relevant group. Garmin, Apple Health and
Android Health Connect are not integrated.

## Device Settings And Security

Appearance, units, selected groups and reminder preferences can be saved.
Native session credentials use secure storage; web sessions use cookies.
Optional biometric unlock uses the operating system: Dinner Swipe does not
receive fingerprint or facial templates. Planning reminders are local device
notifications, enabled with the user's permission.

## Account Data And Deletion Requests

Authenticated users can export data in User settings, Account, Data and legal.
Deletion requests can be submitted there or at
`https://dinner.dcss.dev/delete-account` without an active subscription.
Intake is still manual-review only: a pending receipt does not mean the account
or data has been erased or that billing has been canceled. Shared content,
billing and backup handling need resolution before fulfillment. Contact
`dinnerswipe@dcss.dev` for a pending request or account-access problem.

## App Software Updates

Native builds may check Expo's EAS Update service for compatible app code and assets. The update requests carry network and technical update metadata, including platform, runtime, channel and update identifiers, not the meal, recording, nutrition-log or account-credential payloads handled by Dinner Swipe's backend. Embedded/cached app code remains available for offline startup. Native voice recordings use temporary private app-cache files and cleanup is attempted after transcription, discard or cancellation; an interrupted process can leave cache files until later cleanup or operating-system eviction.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to dinnerswipe@dcss.dev.
