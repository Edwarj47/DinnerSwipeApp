# Dinner Swipe Subscription Terms

Working draft, November 18, 2026. Intended commercial terms, not a published offer
or evidence that Apple, Google or Stripe products have been configured. Review with
the general Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before publication.

## Plans And Complimentary Access

Basic is free. Eligible new verified Dinner Swipe accounts receive 30 consecutive
days of complimentary Premium without entering a payment card or authorizing a
subscription. The eligibility and end date shown in the account are determined by
our server. This welcome access is not an auto-renewing store trial. It ends without
a charge, after which the account continues on Basic unless the user separately
chooses a paid Premium subscription. Usage limits still apply during the welcome
period. Reinstalling the app or restoring a session does not restart it.

Paid Premium's intended U.S. reference price is USD 9.99 per month. The purchasing
store or web checkout shows the actual currency, billing interval, applicable taxes
and total before confirmation. Local prices can differ. We do not apply a single
tax rate to all countries or promise that taxes are always additional rather than
included. A subscription begins only after the user explicitly confirms the payment
provider's purchase flow. Accepting app terms does not authorize payment.

## Renewal, Payment And Access

A paid monthly subscription renews under the terms displayed by the purchasing
provider until cancelled. Payment is charged using the payment method held by that
provider. Renewal timing, billing-retry and grace-period rules depend on the store
and applicable law. We verify store records before granting paid access. Pending,
refunded, revoked, expired or unverified purchases do not independently entitle an
account to Premium. Processing delays or a verification outage may delay an update;
use Restore purchases or contact support rather than purchasing repeatedly.

The account page shows the access currently verified for the signed-in account.
Subscriptions are personal to the Dinner Swipe account to which they are verified
and do not automatically give every member of a group Premium. Your purchasing
store account and Dinner Swipe login can be different accounts; sign in to the
correct one in each service. Cross-store purchases can create separate charges.
Do not buy a second subscription to troubleshoot a restoration problem.

## Cancellation And Purchase Restoration

Manage an Apple purchase in your Apple account's subscription settings and a Google
Play purchase in Google Play's subscription settings. Manage a supported web Stripe
subscription through the web account billing portal. Cancel with the provider that
processed the purchase; uninstalling Dinner Swipe, signing out or deleting your
Dinner Swipe account is not cancellation. Cancellation normally stops future renewal
rather than erasing a charge already made, subject to the provider's terms and law.

Restore purchases checks qualifying purchases with the store and verifies them with
Dinner Swipe. Restoration is not a new purchase and does not restart complimentary
access or transfer a subscription between unrelated Dinner Swipe accounts. Keep
access to the original account. If an original account has been deleted, contact
support and the purchasing provider about the subscription; do not assume a new
Dinner Swipe account will inherit it.

You may delete your Dinner Swipe account without waiting for a subscription to end.
We warn about continuing billing before confirmation, but do not require a
cancellation receipt as a condition of deletion. Account erasure does not erase
records a payment provider must retain under its own obligations.

## Refunds, Consumer Rights And Changes

Refund requests for Apple or Google Play purchases follow the applicable store
process. For web purchases or account-access problems, contact dinnerswipe@dcss.dev
with a receipt identifier, not a card number or password. Refund eligibility and any
withdrawal or cancellation rights depend on the transaction, the provider and
applicable law. Nothing in these terms excludes mandatory consumer rights. Do not
publish a blanket no-refunds or withdrawal-rights waiver.

If price or material subscription terms change, we will provide notice and obtain
any consent required by the purchasing provider or applicable law. Existing paid
Basic subscriptions are not silently cancelled, migrated or repriced by the free
Basic code change. Review those legacy subscriptions separately with the owner and
affected subscribers before making billing changes.
