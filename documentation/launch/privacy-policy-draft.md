# Dinner Swipe Privacy Policy

Status: working draft for owner and legal review, November 18, 2026. Not yet
published or effective. Resolve the review notes below before replacing the
in-app or public policy. This document is not a certification of compliance.

## 1. Who We Are

Dinner Swipe is a meal-planning, recipe, nutrition-tracking and workout application
operated by Data Centric Software Solutions LLC. This policy explains how we
handle information when you use our application, website and related support.
Contact us at dinnerswipe@dcss.dev about privacy, account access or your rights.

Owner review: confirm the legal entity, business mailing address, controller
contact details, intended minimum age, and any required EU representative. Initial
store availability is intended for the United States and European Union; store
territories do not restrict who can reach an internet website.

## 2. Information You Provide

We process your email address, account identifiers, password hash, verification
and consent records, preferences, and communications with support. We also process
content you create, import or save: recipes, ingredients, instructions, source
URLs, photos, meal plans, grocery lists, pantry items and group contributions.
Imports can include filenames, column names, parsed rows, review decisions and
source metadata. Do not upload information about other people without permission.

Nutrition features can include meal dates, portions, manually entered nutrition,
food-database references, notes, goals and targets. Workout features can include
exercise names, templates, planned sessions, sets, repetitions, weights, durations,
completion status and notes. These records, together with allergens or dietary
preferences, can reveal health and fitness information. We do not currently read
Garmin, Apple Health or Android Health Connect records.

## 3. Technical And Usage Information

We process sign-in and session information, IP addresses and user-agent information
where recorded by our systems, request and job identifiers, security events, error
states, usage counters and payment-provider references. The application records
meal and workout interactions such as favorites, choices, votes, skips and undo
actions to provide the requested experience. These records are not an advertising
profile. We do not represent a server log or a pseudonymous identifier as anonymous
when it can still be linked to an account.

The app stores limited data on your device to support authentication, appearance,
offline work and local reminders. Native credentials use secure-storage facilities;
web authentication uses cookies. Optional biometric unlock relies on your device:
we do not receive your fingerprint or facial biometric template. Microphone, photo
and reminder permissions are optional and can be controlled through device settings.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate accounts; provide planning, discovery, tracking,
group and offline functions; deliver requested AI and database tools; maintain
source attribution; verify subscriptions; provide support; and protect the service
against fraud, abuse and unauthorized access. We also use operational information
to diagnose errors and improve reliability. We do not use health or fitness records
to serve targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information.

For users in the EU, the final policy must identify the applicable legal basis for
each purpose. The intended mapping is contract for the service you request,
documented legitimate interests for proportionate security and reliability, legal
obligation where applicable, and consent for optional processing that requires it.
Health-data processing requires a separately assessed Article 9 condition; accepting
general terms is not a substitute for valid explicit consent where required.

Owner/legal review: finalize this mapping, a withdrawal mechanism for any consent
relied upon, and the assessment of relevant U.S. consumer-health-data requirements
before public availability. Do not publish these review notes as completed controls.

## 5. Groups And Shared Content

Content you share with a group is available to that group's members. Owners can
manage available choices and view identified voting information. Member email
addresses, roles and group participation can be visible within group management.
Allergen and dislike matches may appear as group warnings or filters. Consider
this visibility before entering sensitive information or sharing content.

Personal workout performance and nutrition logs remain account-specific unless
you separately share content through a supported feature. Removing an original
shared contribution does not necessarily remove another person's independently
saved copy, personal notes or recorded performance. We assess additional lawful
privacy requests about such independent records separately.

## 6. Optional AI, Voice And Food Database Tools

When you submit a meal description, our server sends it to OpenAI to identify
foods and quantities. When you finish a recording for transcription, the completed
clip is sent through our backend to OpenAI; it is not streamed while you speak.
You review the transcript and food matches before saving. A transcription or draft
does not by itself create a logged meal. AI recipe tools can send your submitted
description, selected image, or relevant extracted recipe-page content to OpenAI.

Food search terms and requested food identifiers are sent through our backend to
fatsecret for nutrition matching. This integration does not send fatsecret your
audio or full meal description. Client credentials remain server-side. Returned
nutrition and serving descriptions are temporary; saved calculations retain
permitted food and serving identifiers, your labels and portions, and manually
entered nutrition. Refreshed values can change and can be unavailable offline.

These tools require the current optional nutrition agreement where applicable.
Acceptance is recorded by revision and time; it does not start recording or submit
content. Avoid including unnecessary personal or sensitive information in prompts,
photos and recordings. Providers process submissions under their applicable terms
and data-processing arrangements. We do not promise zero provider retention or
that all provider records disappear immediately when you delete an account.

## 7. Service Providers And Other Disclosures

We use infrastructure, database, storage, email, software-delivery, AI, nutrition
and payment providers to operate Dinner Swipe. Optional native updates use Expo's
delivery service, which receives network and update-related technical information,
not your meal logs or audio through this delivery integration. Recipe-source and
retailer links lead to independently operated sites with their own policies.

Apple or Google handles native subscription checkout and payment details. Stripe
handles supported web checkout. We receive subscription identifiers, product and
status information, verification evidence and relevant billing events; Dinner Swipe
does not directly store full payment-card details. We may disclose information to
professional advisers or authorities when reasonably necessary to comply with law,
respond to valid legal process or protect people and systems. Sharing with service
providers is not permission for unrelated advertising use.

Owner review: publish an accurate provider/subprocessor list, processing locations,
contractual safeguards and EU international-transfer mechanism. Do not claim all
data stays in the EU, that every provider is an independent controller, or that a
transfer mechanism is in place without verifying the relevant contracts and services.

## 8. Retention

Account content is generally retained while needed to provide your account and
requested features, subject to deletion and the exceptions below. Fatsecret
response caching is limited to less than 24 hours; current configuration uses
23 hours. Food and serving identifiers may be retained independently of cached
nutrition. Temporary server audio-conversion files are removed after processing;
standalone transcripts have a short in-memory retry cache of up to five minutes.
Submitting transcript text into a meal draft changes the relevant retention:
draft-access expiry is not the same as automatic deletion of stored job metadata.

Native recordings use private device cache files with attempted cleanup after
submission, cancellation or discard. An operating-system interruption can leave
temporary files until a later cleanup. Ordinary sign-out can retain account-bound
offline drafts; explicit account erasure clears the deleting device's account cache
and pending changes. We cannot instantly erase a device that is disconnected.

Some limited records may remain for legally required accounting, fraud prevention,
security, deletion suppression and resolving disputes. We restrict these records
and do not use them to reconstruct a deleted profile. The accompanying retention
schedule must be approved and operationally enforced before promising specific
backup or legal-retention deadlines. No backup-expiry deadline is established by
this draft alone.

## 9. Export, Correction And Account Deletion

You can use User settings, Account, Data and legal to export data or initiate
account deletion. The external account-deletion page is
https://dinner.dcss.dev/delete-account. If you cannot access your account, contact
dinnerswipe@dcss.dev. We verify identity proportionately before acting on a request.

The new deletion workflow, once released and enabled, revokes account access,
erases private account records and removes original recipes and workouts you
shared. Other users' independent copies and personal records are preserved;
unavailable shared sources may leave a neutral history placeholder. Ownership of
a shared group must first be transferred, or a sole-member group left using its
existing controls. A failed cleanup is reported as pending, not as completed.

Deleting an account or uninstalling the app does not cancel an external subscription
or create a refund. Manage renewal through the purchasing provider. You can still
confirm deletion without waiting for the paid period to end. A deleted account's
purchase history is not automatically transferable to a new Dinner Swipe account.

Current-release note: until fulfillment is deployed and enabled, the existing
deletion form submits a manual-review request rather than immediately erasing the
account. Remove this release note only after live acceptance testing and update the
published policy to match the actual deployed behavior.

## 10. Your Rights And Security

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, portability
or restriction; object to certain processing; withdraw relevant consent; and complain
to a competent supervisory authority. Contact us using the address above. We explain
any lawful limitation and do not make payment of a subscription a condition of a
privacy request. Withdrawal does not undo processing that was lawful before it.

We use access controls, authentication, encryption in transit, restricted credentials
and operational safeguards. No system is risk-free. We assess and respond to security
incidents and applicable notification obligations. Dinner Swipe is not a medical
provider or an emergency service, and this policy does not claim HIPAA certification.
